Viking 21a Basic Stitch Cam by maxkrippler on Thingiverse: https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:6018240

Summary:
This cam for the Husqvarna Viking model 21a sewing machine will produce the two most basic stitches used for garment construction-- Zigzag stitches and 3-step zigzag stitches. This will save you from wearing out your vintage cams, or replace a vintage cam that has already worn down from years of use.  Positions 1&amp;2 produce a three-step zigzag, and positions 3~5 produce a normal zigzag stitch.
